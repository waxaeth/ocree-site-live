About Ocree

Real estate knowledge. Regulatory discipline. Technology that earns its place.

Our story

Built from experience in the market

Ocree's founders spent more than two decades running brokerages and closing commercial real estate transactions.

They saw how much of private real estate still depended on phone calls, spreadsheets and personal introductions. Ocree started in Canada, working with regulators from the beginning. Commercial real estate is our first asset class.

Mission

Make private assets easier to distribute well

Ocree provides the infrastructure that lets private assets reach investors through a consistent, regulated process. We start with commercial real estate, combining OARDS review, digital securities and distribution through registered dealers.

Vision

Private markets that connect as efficiently as public markets

With the same standard of disclosure. Built in Canada, designed to extend to private credit, private funds and other asset classes.

How we build

Three disciplines, one platform

Ocree combines product knowledge, regulatory understanding and practical technology instead of treating them as separate workstreams.

Technology

Digital issuance, registry and reporting that make each transaction faster to complete.

Product knowledge

We understand what owners need to raise capital and what dealers need to offer it.

Compliance first

Every step is designed with regulatory oversight in mind.

Core values

Transparency, security and compliance. Technology only where it makes the process clearer or faster.
