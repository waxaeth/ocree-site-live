About Ocree

Built from experience in the market

Ocree's founders spent more than two decades running brokerages and closing commercial real estate transactions.

They saw how much of private real estate still depended on phone calls, spreadsheets and personal introductions.

Ocree started in Canada, working with regulators from the beginning. Commercial real estate is our first asset class.

Mission

Ocree connects private asset investments with investors through a consistent, regulated process. We start with commercial real estate, combining OARDS review, digital securities and distribution through registered dealers.

Vision

Private markets that connect as efficiently as public markets, with the same standard of disclosure. Built in Canada, designed to extend to private equity and credit, private funds and other asset classes.

How we build

Technology

Digital issuance, registry and reporting that make each transaction faster to complete.

Product knowledge

We understand what owners need to raise capital and what dealers need to offer it.

Compliance first

Every step is designed with regulatory compliance in mind.

Core values

Every private deal tells its story differently. We make sure no one has to read between the lines.

Meet the team

Meet the team

Ted Davis is the Co-Founder and CEO of Ocree, a digital infrastructure platform helping modernize private markets. After more than 20 years spanning commercial real estate, capital markets, and entrepreneurship, Ted saw firsthand how outdated processes create friction for investors, issuers, and advisors.

Driven by a passion for innovation and market accessibility, he co-founded Ocree to help bridge traditional finance and emerging technology, building infrastructure that makes private markets more efficient, transparent and consistent.

Having participated in more than $5 billion of commercial real estate transactions, Ted is focused on creating a future where investing in private assets is as seamless and accessible as investing in public markets, while maintaining a strong compliance-first foundation.

"I believe private markets are entering a digital evolution. Ocree's mission is to provide the infrastructure that helps unlock greater access, efficiency and confidence across the entire investment ecosystem."

Ryan Wilkinson is a Co-Founder of Ocree and President of Ocree Properties, bringing over 20 years of experience in commercial real estate, investment analysis, transaction structuring, and business development across North American and international markets. Ryan has extensive experience in asset evaluation, due diligence, market intelligence, risk assessment, and value creation.

As President of Ocree Properties, Ryan leads the company's real estate strategy, including asset origination, investment evaluation, strategic partnerships, and identifying opportunities across private real estate markets.

Ryan is focused on combining institutional real estate expertise with modernizing the financial infrastructure to create greater access, transparency, efficiency, and confidence across private markets.

"Private markets represent an enormous opportunity. By combining deep real estate expertise with modern financial infrastructure, we can create a more transparent, efficient, and accessible investment ecosystem."

Anne is a member of the CSC Fintech Advisory Forum. She works with regulators and industry to modernize capital markets rules in ways that support innovation, protect investors and maintain confidence in the markets.
