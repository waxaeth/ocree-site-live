Ocree Capital Inc. receives regulatory approval to operate as exempt market dealer in Ontario, Alberta, and British Columbia

Ocree will revolutionize the way real estate assets are bought and sold.

Toronto, Ontario - Ocree Capital Inc. ("Ocree Capital") today announces that it has recently received regulatory approval to operate as an exempt market dealer from the Ontario, Alberta, and British Columbia Securities Commissions, making Ocree Capital one of the first entities in these jurisdictions to provide a streamlined, efficient alternative to traditional capital market real estate transactions.

"We're excited to introduce Ocree Capital and its affiliates ("Ocree") to these three provinces and we look forward to exploring future opportunities in other markets," says Ted Davis, CEO. "The Ocree platform transforms traditional real estate assets into digital assets, revolutionizing the way real estate is bought and sold - and every investment transaction is completed in a secure, transparent, and tamper-proof manner."

Empowered by technology, Ocree's expertly skilled team is committed to relentlessly drive innovation and efficiency for clients. Ocree offers a proprietary platform specializing in the issuance and sale of securities representing ownership in commercial real estate assets and utilizing blockchain technology. The platform provides an efficient, transparent process for real estate transactions.

Real estate owners have liquidity and flexibility with their properties, while investors have an accessible and automated platform.

Although traditional real estate transactions and investments can be inefficient and manual, Ocree automates the process. With Ocree, investors will have partial ownership in real estate assets. Once the assets are reviewed and due diligence is completed, securities are offered for sale by Ocree Capital, as a regulated exempt market dealer, to accredited investors through Ocree's proprietary platform.

Increasing demand has driven this type of investment, particularly around the ability to invest in Real World Assets (RWA) such as real estate.

Ocree's leadership team has a combined 50+ years in commercial real estate, with involvement in more than $5B worth of transactions. The team is also comprised of experienced professionals from the security, banking, operations, compliance, and technology sectors.

About Ocree Capital

Ocree Capital Inc. is an exempt market dealer in Ontario, Alberta and British Columbia. Ocree Capital, together with its affiliated companies, operates a proprietary platform offering fractionalized real estate assets.
