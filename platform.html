Technology in the background

Each offering is issued as a digital security, with ownership recorded on a blockchain-based registry. This is often called tokenization.

Issuers, dealers and investors follow familiar regulated processes.

A shared registry supports clear ownership records and reporting.

Technology is used where it makes the process clearer or faster.
