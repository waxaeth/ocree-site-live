Curious about private real estate?

Investors may access eligible offerings through their own dealer or through Ocree Capital, subject to the applicable exemption and their circumstances.

Why private real estate

Who can invest

Eligibility depends on the exemption used for an offering and the investor's circumstances.

What you're investing in

Each offering comes with its own documents, terms and resale options.

How to invest

Understand risk
