Eligible investors

Private real estate, through a registered dealer

Most private offerings in Canada are available to accredited investors under National Instrument 45-106. Investors may access eligible offerings through their own dealer or through Ocree Capital.

Who can invest

Eligibility depends on the exemption used for an offering and the investor's circumstances.

How to invest

Speak with your existing dealer or connect with Ocree Capital.

Understand risk

Private real estate is illiquid and can lose value. Review the offering documents carefully.
