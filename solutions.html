Eligible investors

Curious about private real estate?

Most private offerings in Canada are available to accredited investors under National Instrument 45-106. Investors may access eligible offerings through their own dealer or through Ocree Capital.

Why private real estate

Some investors look beyond stocks and bonds to spread their holdings across more types of assets.

Who can invest

Eligibility depends on the exemption used for an offering and the investor's circumstances.

What you're investing in

Each offering comes with its own documents, terms and resale options. Your dealer walks you through them and confirms the fit.

How to invest

Speak with your existing dealer or connect with Ocree Capital.

Understand risk
