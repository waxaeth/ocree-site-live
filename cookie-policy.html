Cookie Policy 

Effective Date: August 2024 

Ocree Corporation and its affiliates ("Ocree", "We", "Us", or "Our") use cookies and similar technologies on our website (the “Website”) to collect automated information when you visit our Website. By using our Website you agree to the placement of cookies on your device in accordance with the terms of this Policy. 

For further information about our privacy practices, please see our Privacy Notice. 

What are Cookies? 

Cookies are small text files created on your computer or device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device and store some information about your preferences or past actions. Similar technologies such as clear GIFs, web beacons, and pixel tags also function this way. In this policy, we use the term 'cookies' to refer to all these technologies. All cookies have an owner which tells you who the cookie belongs to. 

Cookies are commonly used to make websites work more efficiently for the user, as well as to provide information to the site owners. They help us to provide you with a better user experience and to improve our Website and services. 

Types of Cookies We Use 

Essential Cookies 

  • Type: Session Cookies 

  • Administered by: Ocree and its affiliates 

  • Purpose: These cookies are essential for you to browse our Website and use its features. They enable basic functions like page navigation and authentication for access to secure areas of the Website. The Website cannot function properly without these cookies. These cookies are erased when you close your browser. 

Targeting and Advertising Cookies 

  • Type: Persistent Cookies 

  • Administered by: Third Parties 

  • Purpose: These cookies collect information about your browsing habits to deliver advertisements that are more relevant to you and your interests. They also help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. These cookies stay in one of your browser’s subfolders until you delete them or your browser deletes them based on the expiration date contained within the cookie’s file.    

Managing and Deleting Cookies 

You can manage or delete cookies through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. Here are links to manage cookie settings on popular browsers: 

  • Chrome: Google Chrome Help 

  • Internet Explorer: Microsoft Support 

  • Firefox: Mozilla Support 

  • Safari: Apple Support 

For other browsers, please refer to the official web pages of the respective browser. 

Disabling cookies may reduce your ability to take advantage of the features of our Website. 

Opting Out of Interest-Based Advertising 

For more information about interest-based advertising and to understand your options for opting out, you can visit the Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada (DAAC) website at DAAC YourAdChoices. 

Important Notes: 

  • Opting out of interest-based advertising does not mean you will not see ads online. It means the ads you see may be less relevant to you. 

  • Opt-outs are managed through cookies, so if you delete your cookies, you will need to opt out again. 

  • You need to opt out separately for each browser and device you use. 

Caution Regarding Automatic Cookie Functionality 

Browsers such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Internet Explorer and others, allow you to store passwords and log-in information so you do not need to re-enter this information manually each time you access websites. We strongly urge you not to use this functionality to protect your personal details and prevent unauthorized use of any online accounts you may have.
